Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our tools work and what data (if any) is collected.

Data Processing

All processing happens entirely in your browser. When you use our tools:

  • No files or data are uploaded to any server
  • All conversions, generations, and processing happen locally on your device
  • We cannot see, access, or store any of your data

Local Storage

We use localStorage only to remember your language preference (English or Bahasa Melayu). No other data is stored.

Analytics

We do not use analytics, tracking cookies, or third-party scripts. Your browsing activity is not tracked.

Third-Party Services

Our tools do not connect to any third-party services or APIs. Everything runs offline in your browser.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
